# Karhunkierros


Why i go for hike or just walk many kilometers, i wonder on this many times, but couldn't find reliable anwsers yet. What questions I am trying to answer and what void in myself i want to fill up. One thing, I know for sure is that whenever i go for long walk in forest, I feel more alive. May be to experience this liveness again, I jumped on a train to Kemijarvi in Lapand, and started 82 km of hike from Hautajarvi to Ruka. Surronded by almost 80 bears in the region, lot of reindeers, I started my walk passing through the Oulanka National Park along the Oulanka river where once hunter gatherers used to roam during the stone age surviving on fish from river, wild reindeer, elk and bears. Bears were also spirtually significant among Fennoscandian hunter gatherers assoicated with Peijaiset ceremony intented to appease the bear's spirit so it woudn't seek revenge or bring bad luck to future hunts. In the national park there are wilderness huts where people can spend night. I put up my tent near the lake beside the wilderness hut. Some of these huts are restored from the reinder herders cabins. Walking again the next day, passing along the river and wintenessing the beauty of forest, rivers, rapids, and lakes, my mind roams around thinking about those stone age hunters, reindeers herders, and me 21st century human walking on that land.

By sitting near a rapid, I thought of my presence in this cosmos by zooming myself out and realizing again how insignificant i am in this vast universe sitting on a stone in large forest on a pale blue dot in vast milkyway. My day to day worries goes away whenever i think this way and see insignificance of my self in this cosmos. I realized that many things are just in our heads, and the nature doesn't care about that. The complexity of mind creates problems biggers then they actually are. Some wise men said once something like all human problems originate from man's inability to sit quietly at one place alone. Our mind wants continues triggers and things to process. Putting your mind at rest is the skill that yogies and sufis spend years to acheive. As fellow hikers pass by and ah my mind comes back. I stand up using my Asa (walking stick which i collected from forest. It symbolises we as a wanderer in this world) and started walking again.

I met some people on the way and asked them what is happiness. One fellow traveler told me that someone said happiness comes when there is purpose, joyfullness and satisfaction. One traveller said happiness comes when there is no problem in life. One said they are happy because they live in a safe environment and have good career. One said they are happy because they live pretty close to nature. I wonder how can we measure happiness. Is it tied to what we have, or external things or is it a state of mind, a matter not of how much we hold, but of how little we crave?. I think of those faqirs, qalanders, and yogis who hold nothing and chase nothing. I think of Diogenes saying to Alexander to stand out of sunlight as favour instead of asking for any thing, a man who has already arrived at enough. I think of Shams Tabriz, the wandering dervish who owned nothing and belonged nowhere, yet burned with a joy so full that it set Rumi alight and turned a scholar into a poet of love. I think of a traveller on Camino De Santiago walking from Rome to Santiago with little in this pockets, a wide smile on his face and very powerful positive and blissful energy. I think of a qalandar sitting on a train arriving at Lahore Data Darbar with no shoes, and a khirqa, and a potli (small bundle) with a big smile and wonderul energy with him. And then I think of people who lack for nothing, who have more than they could ever need, and yet live in misery inside their own minds.

Infront of Jussinkamppa, I cooked Rajma Chawal (Rice and beans) and sat there for a while with floating state of mind staring at the lake and watching people stopping for break and passing by. I continued walking, days passing by, meeting fellow travellers and approaching the Ruka. Anteeks, missa on ruoka kauppa. I asked couple of kids fixing their bike about where can i find grocery store. Toisella puolella on ruokakauppa, The kid told me about grocery store in Ruka village on the other side of tuntori (hill) and offered me to show the way. He started walking with me as we were passing the tuntori (hill). The kid was curious about my journey and asked about the trail and journey. As we approach the village, I said kittos (thanks) for this help and he cycled back. The night was approaching and the first bus to Kuusamo was leaving at 04:30 in morning. Kuusamo is just 26 km away, why not just walk, a thought comes to my mind, so i started walking to Kuusamo while enjoying the rain. Saw few reindeers passing by. Surrendly a car stopped and a man asked me to come join him. I saw there were two fellow travellers with him in the car as well, whom I met on the trail. The man gave me an offer of Sauna which i couldn't refuse. He was a lovely happy man, full of happiness, joy and laugh. He lives deep in country side. He told us about a place where bear has recently eaten an Elk. The bones are scattered around as we were standing on the site. Look this is bear poop, told the man. How we can recognize it, we said. It's similar to human poop but with full stomach pointing to big poop. Hahahah, we just launghed. We went for a tradional Finnish wood heated sauna. The man told me about country side, he was full of joy and we launghed around as we talk.
